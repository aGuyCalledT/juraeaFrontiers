## Welcome to the continent

The ancient mists have parted, revealing the untouched and resource-rich continent of **Juräa**. For centuries, the old world has been dying, its lands stripped bare and it's magic fading. Now, the war is upon all of them. 

*Chronicles of Juraea* is a deeply strategic, turn-based colonization game played entirely within your terminal or console. Command your settlers, manage resources, engage in tactical grid-based combat, and conquer the new world. But beware—you are not the only ones seeking a new home.

## The Factions

Three distinct powers have landed on the shores of Juräa. Who will you lead to dominance?

### The Death Cults
> *"What is dead may never die! The soil of this new world will be fertile with the bones of our enemies. Blessed be Solomon."*

![Death Cults Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/600x200/2b0000/ffffff?text=[+HUMAN+NECROMANCER+ART+PLACEHOLDER+])

Driven to the brink of extinction in the old world, a radical sect of humanity turned to the dark arts for survival. The Death Cults are led by powerful Necromancers who view life as a temporary state. 
*   **Playstyle:** Swarm tactics, resurrecting fallen units (both friend and foe) directly on the battlefield, and utilizing dark magic to wither enemy resources.
*   **Strengths:** Numbers, attrition warfare, cheap unit replenishment.

### The Mountain Guilds (Dwarves)
> *"This land was gifted to the children of Mammon. We shall claim it as our own."*

![Trade Clans Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/600x200/4a3f35/ffffff?text=[+DWARVEN+TRADE+CLAN+ART+PLACEHOLDER+])

The Dwarven Trade Clans view the colonization of Juraea as the ultimate business venture. They bring with them unparalleled engineering, heavy armor, and a deep understanding of resource logistics.
*   **Playstyle:** Defensive, economic powerhouse. Building heavily fortified settlements and fielding slow but nearly indestructible units.
*   **Strengths:** Resource generation, defensive sieges, heavy melee combat.

### Evergarden (Elves)
> *"We do not conquer the land; we grow with it. Juräa belongs to Sylvana, and we are its guardians."*

![Evergarden Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/600x200/103a14/ffffff?text=[+ELVEN+EVERGARDEN+ART+PLACEHOLDER+])

Fleeing the corruption of their ancestral woods, the Elves of the Evergarden seek to turn Juräa into a massive, interconnected living forest. They are deeply attuned to nature and magic.
*   **Playstyle:** High mobility, ranged superiority, and terrain manipulation. They can grow the "Evergarden" across the map to buff their units and hinder enemies.
*   **Strengths:** Speed, ranged attacks, map control via natural overgrowth.
